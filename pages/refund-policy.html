Refund Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

In short: points you bought and haven't used can be refunded within 7 days. Points you've spent can't be refunded, but many things on the site return your points automatically (lost bids, cancelled waiting-list spots, declined offers, cancelled billboard weeks).

1. Refunds of purchased points

You can get a refund when

  • you ask within 7 days of the payment, and
  • the points from that payment have not been used.

If you used part of a purchase, the unused part can be refunded at the rate you paid. Points are counted as used in the order they were received, with free and bonus points used first.

We also refund

  • duplicate charges or charges where points were never added because of a technical error;
  • unused purchased points if we shut down the service.

We can't refund

  • points already spent on spots, extensions, winning bids, billboard weeks, reservation deposits that were kept, or HD photo unlocks;
  • free points, sign-up bonuses, invite rewards and other bonus points;
  • points received from selling a spot;
  • points taken back or spent on a spot removed for breaking the Terms.

2. Automatic point returns

These are returned to your wallet right away, without asking us:

  • Outbid in an auction: your held bid comes back as soon as someone bids higher.
  • Waiting list: your deposit comes back in full if you cancel before your turn. If your turn comes and you don't buy within 48 hours, the deposit is kept.
  • Offers: the offered points come back if the holder declines, if you cancel, or after 7 days without an answer.
  • Billboard bookings: full refund in points if you cancel at least 48 hours before the booked week starts.

3. How to ask for a refund

  1. Email youngs.gapp@gmail.com from the email address of your account.
  2. Include your PayPal order ID (from your PayPal receipt or the error message) and a short reason.
  3. We review it within 3 business days. The matching points are removed from your wallet when the refund is approved.
  4. The money goes back to your original PayPal payment method. PayPal usually shows it within 5–10 days.

Refunds can only be sent to the original payment method.

4. Didn't get your points?

If PayPal charged you but your points haven't appeared, wait a few minutes and refresh the page. Payments that didn't finish in your browser are completed automatically once PayPal notifies us. If the points still aren't there after an hour, email us with your PayPal order ID and we'll add them.

5. Chargebacks and disputes

Please contact us before opening a PayPal dispute or a card chargeback. We can usually fix the problem faster. If a payment is reversed or charged back, the matching points and anything bought with them may be removed, and the account may be suspended until it's resolved.

6. Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any refund rights you have under the consumer laws of your country.

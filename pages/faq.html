Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Getting started

What is World Advertising Map?

A world map where every country, state and province is a "spot" you can hold for a while. While you hold a spot you can paint pixel art on it, add an HD photo, and attach a link to your website, shop or social page. Everyone who visits the map can see it.

Holding a spot is a right to use a space on our platform. It is not ownership of real land, and it is not an investment.

Do I get anything for signing up?

Yes. New accounts get 50 pt for free, which is enough to hold one of the cheapest spots for a week.

How do I buy points?

Log in, open Wallet and pay with PayPal (PayPal balance or card). Points are added right after the payment.

Can I invite friends?

Yes. Share your invite link from the menu. When a friend signs up with your link and makes their first PayPal top-up, you get 100 pt and your friend gets 50 pt. The link must be used within 7 days of the friend signing up.

Holding a spot

How long do I keep a spot?

When you buy, you pick a period. Nobody can take the spot from you during that period.

PeriodPrice
1 weekBase price
1 month3.5 × weekly price (about 18% off)
1 year30 × weekly price (about 42% off)
Lifetime100 × weekly price
  • You can extend any time before your period ends. We remind you 3 days before it ends.
  • After it ends, there is a 3-day grace period where only you can extend.
  • After the grace period, people on the waiting list get the first chance to buy. If nobody is waiting, anyone can buy it or start an auction.
  • A lifetime spot stays yours unless you sell it yourself or it is removed for breaking the rules.
How are prices set?

The weekly price depends on population, major cities and economic size, and is adjusted by auction results. The cheapest spots start at 50 pt per week (10 pt = $1).

What do the spot status labels mean?
  • Available: nobody holds it. You can buy it now.
  • Taken: someone holds it until the date shown.
  • Lifetime: held permanently.
  • Grace period: the period ended. Only the holder can extend for 3 days.
  • Reserved: the next person on the waiting list has 48 hours to buy it.
  • Open: the period and grace period are over. Anyone can buy it or start an auction.
  • Billboard: a popular spot that is booked by the week instead of held.
Can I lose my spot?

Not during the period you paid for. A spot only changes hands when:

  • your period and the 3-day grace period end without an extension;
  • someone outbids you in a next-period auction (see below);
  • you accept an offer or start a sale auction yourself;
  • it is removed for breaking our Terms.

Getting a spot someone else holds

The spot I want is taken. What can I do?
  • 👀 Watch: free. We tell you when it becomes available.
  • 🎟️ Join the waiting list: pay a deposit of 10% of the weekly price (at least 10 pt). The deposit counts toward the price when it's your turn. When your turn comes you have 48 hours to buy. Cancel before your turn and the deposit comes back in full. If you miss your 48-hour window, the deposit is not returned. Up to 10 people can wait per spot.
  • 💬 Make an offer: the points you offer are held. If the holder accepts, the spot is yours. If they decline or don't answer within 7 days, the points come back automatically.
What is a next-period auction?

In the last 7 days of someone's period (not lifetime), anyone can start an auction for the next period. It ends exactly when the current period ends. The current holder can keep the spot by matching the highest bid. A tie goes to the current holder.

How do auctions work?
  • Bids are whole points and must beat the current highest bid.
  • Your bid is held from your wallet. If someone outbids you, it is returned right away.
  • A bid in the last 5 minutes extends the auction to 5 minutes from that bid, so nobody can "snipe" at the last second.
  • The highest bidder wins when the auction ends.
Can I sell my spot?

Yes. Accept an offer or start a sale auction from your spot's panel. You receive 90% of the sale price in points; 10% is the platform fee. You can't bid on your own sale.

How do billboards work?

Some very popular spots are billboards. Instead of holding them, you book whole weeks (up to 12 weeks ahead) on the calendar. You can cancel a booking up to 48 hours before its week starts for a full refund in points.

Decorating your spot

How do I draw pixel art?

Open your spot and press the pixel editor button. Pick colors and click to paint. Your work saves automatically. You can also stamp an image and it is converted into pixels.

What are HD photos?

You can upload a sharp photo that is cut to the exact shape of your spot and shown on the map when people zoom in. Files are converted in your browser to a small image (max 1.5 MB).

  • Free if your current period is 1 month or longer, lifetime, or a billboard booking.
  • Otherwise you can unlock it once for your current period. The price is half the weekly price (at least 50 pt) and is shown before you pay.
  • When the spot changes hands, the HD photo is removed.
Can I add a link to my website?

Yes. Add a title and a link in your spot's panel. Visitors can open it from the map, and you can see how many clicks it got. Links to scams, malware or illegal content are removed.

Can I share my spot?

Yes. Each spot has a share link with a preview image, which works well on social media and messengers.

Payments and refunds

Can I turn points back into money?

No. Points can only be used on World Advertising Map and can't be exchanged for cash. This is not a financial product, cryptocurrency or investment.

Can I get a refund?

Points you bought and haven't used can be refunded within 7 days of the purchase. Points already spent, and free or bonus points, can't be refunded. See the Refund Policy for details.

I paid but didn't get my points.

Wait a few minutes and refresh. Most delayed payments are added automatically. If they still don't appear, email us with your PayPal order ID and we'll fix it.

Safety and rules

What is not allowed?

Sexual content, violence or gore, hate speech, content that infringes copyright or trademarks, scams, spam links, malware, illegal activity, and sharing other people's private information. See the Terms of Service.

How do I report something?

Open the spot and press Report. Our team reviews every report and can remove the artwork, photo or link, or take back the spot.
